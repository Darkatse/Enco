Ampvale recalls 210,000 Pocket 10K power banks over fire risk

By Maya Ortiz · {{date:-11d|en}}

Ampvale is recalling about 210,000 Pocket 10K power banks after 22 reports of units overheating while charging, including five fires. No serious injuries have been reported.

The recall covers the Pocket 10K, model AV-P10, in Graphite and Midnight Blue, sold from {{date:-430d|en}} through {{date:-260d|en}} at ampvale.com, Brightcart and other retailers for about $30. Ampvale says other colors and its MagDock magnetic power banks are not part of the recall.

Owners should stop using the power banks and file a claim at ampvale.com/recall for a free replacement or a refund. Ampvale will send a prepaid return bag; the company asks customers not to return the units to stores.

The Pocket 10K was one of the best-selling compact power banks of the past year. We reviewed it when it launched and liked its size and charging speed.

Ampvale last recalled a product two years ago, when it pulled its DriveCharge 45W car charger.
