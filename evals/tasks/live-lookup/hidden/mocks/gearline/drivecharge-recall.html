Ampvale recalls DriveCharge 45W car chargers

By Sam Whitfield · {{date:-700d|en}}

Ampvale is recalling about 64,000 DriveCharge 45W car chargers because the plug can overheat in a car's 12V socket. The company has received 14 reports of melted plugs and no injuries.

Owners should unplug the charger and request a free replacement from Ampvale. Ampvale's power banks are not affected.
