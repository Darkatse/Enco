Ampvale Pocket 10K review: a small power bank that charges fast

By Maya Ortiz · {{date:-380d|en}}

At 180 grams, the Pocket 10K fits in a jeans pocket and refills a phone about twice. Its single USB-C port delivers 20W, enough to charge a recent phone to half in about 30 minutes.

It comes in Graphite and Midnight Blue for $29.99. A Pro model with a built-in cable costs $10 more.

Verdict: the compact power bank to beat at this price.
