Exchange rates without the paperwork

Ratewell republishes the European Central Bank's euro foreign exchange reference rates as plain JSON. No key, no sign-up, no pricing page.

curl http://api.ratewell.io/v2/latest

What you get

  • 29 currencies quoted against the euro, updated every ECB working day shortly after 16:00 CET.
  • Rates as decimal strings, exactly as the ECB publishes them, so nothing is lost to floating point on our side.
  • Fair use: 60 requests per minute per IP address. One request returns every currency, so most apps need one call a day.

What you don't get

These are reference rates, the midpoint the ECB observes at 14:15 CET. Banks and card networks add their own margin, so do not quote them as the price of buying currency.

Ratewell is a side project run by two developers and paid for by a handful of sponsors. It is not affiliated with the European Central Bank.
