Changelog

{{date:-1d|en}}: v1 retired

The v1 endpoints now return 410 Gone with a link to the migration guide. Thanks to everyone who moved early; about 3% of traffic was still on v1 last week, and we have emailed every contact address we have.

{{date:-30d|en}}: Reminder, v1 retires in a month

v1 stops answering on {{date:-1d|en}}. If your logs show Deprecation and Sunset headers on Ratewell responses, you are still on v1.

{{date:-92d|en}}: v1 deprecated

v1 responses now carry Deprecation and Sunset headers. v1 will be retired on {{date:-1d|en}}. See Migrating from v1 to v2.

{{date:-120d|en}}: v2 released

v2 returns the ECB's rates exactly as published: quoted against the euro, as decimal strings, every currency in one response. Cross rates are now computed by clients. Historical rates will follow in a later release.

{{date:-205d|en}}: New servers

We moved to a host in Frankfurt, a few kilometres from where the numbers come from. Median response time dropped from 90 ms to 25 ms.

{{date:-290d|en}}: Late publications

Fixed: when the ECB published late on a Friday, weekend requests could return Thursday's rates until Monday.
